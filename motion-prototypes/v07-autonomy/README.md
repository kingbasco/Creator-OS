# V07 — Autonomy scale opening prototype

18-second silent prototype for **What Should AI Do — and What Should You Still Review?**

Scene 1 places coding tasks along a reversible → sensitive autonomy scale. Scene 2 demonstrates the central guardrail: a destructive database action physically stops at a human approval gate, opens a diff/impact panel, and only continues after approval.

The tone is balanced: more autonomy where work is easy to review and undo, stronger review where impact or privilege increases.

No narration or paid AI generation is used in this prototype.
